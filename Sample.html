CloudFlare WARP InstructionsAAC Texas Windows

  • Download Cloudflare WARP for Windows. https://one.one.one.one/
  • Open the downloaded installer and follow the setup steps.
  • Once installed, WARP will launch automatically and appear in your system tray.
  • Click Next and accept Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Launch Cloudflare on your device.
  • When prompted to sign in, enter your full angstorm-usa.com email address.
  • For the Location or gateway dropdown, select AAC-Texas Gateway.
  • Complete MFA when prompted, once connected your IP will route through AAC- Texas mode.
  • Turn on the WARP toggle to connect.
  • You are all set - WARP is now running and helping protect your internet connection.
